How Applications are Reviewed
We aim for a mix of technical backgrounds and domains across participants. Applicants should best showcase their technical skills in the application form. 

We review every application against two criteria, using only your answers on the application form:

Your project idea: how clear and feasible your proposed project is, and how well it fits one of the two project themes.

Your technical skill: the technical skill shown in the AI project you describe, including what you built and the techniques you used. Links to your GitHub, LinkedIn or a shipped product help us verify your experience and skill but are not required.

Both criteria are weighted equally. Applications are evaluated on a rolling basis. Eligible applications meeting minimum scoring cut-offs will be accepted automatically, up to the first 20,000 acceptances. Eligible applications that do not meet the minimum scoring cut-offs will be accepted based on score received, if space allows. Event Overview and Event Waiver
All individuals interested in attending the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon (the “Event”) must complete, submit an application through the official application process, and be accepted. Applications must be submitted by October 25, 2026 at 11:59PM EST to be considered for participation in the virtual event – participation is virtual only. Participation is limited to a maximum of 20,000 accepted applicants. Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis as they're received. Once spots are filled, the application will close. All fields in the event application form must be completed accurately and truthfully. Incomplete or false information may result in disqualification. 

Submission of an application does not guarantee attendance at the Event. The Organizers reserve the right to change or cancel the Event at any time.

Event Overview

Participants build with Meta AI products, including Muse Spark, Glimmer, SAM 3, and any other Meta AI products identified on the Event page.

Participants selected to participate in the Hackathon will be provided $150 in Meta Model API credits for development over a ten (10) day period.

Participants will submit their project to Cerebral Valley’s portal at https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit.

Finalists will be chosen by a panel of judges. The judges’ selections are final and binding.

Hackathon Judging Criteria. These criteria apply only to projects submitted during the hackathon, not to applications. Applications are reviewed as described in 'How Applications are Reviewed’. Hackathon Project submissions will be evaluated based on the following criteria: Impact (30%) — the real-world potential of the Project, who benefits, how much it matters, whether it could become something people use, and whether it fits one of the problem statements; Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%) — how creatively the team used Meta's AI tooling, whether they went beyond a basic integration, and whether they surfaced unexpected capabilities; Technical Depth & Execution (20%) — whether the engineering is sound and thoughtfully refined and the Project demonstrates real craft; and Demo (25%) — whether the Project has a working, impressive demo, whether the product holds up live, and the quality of the demo video.

There are two (2) tracks. A participant may apply for one (1) track only, and a project submission may be entered in one (1) track only.

Indie Developer track. Open to individual builders and students, teams of up to four (4), subject to the eligibility requirements below. Each team shall designate one participant to submit the project on behalf of the team. The designated participant shall identify all other team members in the project submission. 

Startup track. Open to technical founders building a product. The team designates one participant as the contact for notices and prize payment. 

If selected to participate in the Hackathon, finalists may receive prizes in U.S. dollars or local currency equivalent, at Organizer’s sole discretion. Prizes are awarded only after eligibility verification and compliance with these terms. These prizes may include:

Indie Developer Track, total $400,000:

1st Prize: $150,000

2nd Prize: $100,000

3rd Prize: $75,000

4th Prize: $50,000

5th Prize: $25,000

Startup Track, total $550,000:

1st Prize: $250,000

2nd Prize: $150,000

3rd Prize: $75,000

4th Prize: $50,000

5th Prize: $25,000

Bonus Prizes. The Organizers may award additional prizes of up to $50,000 in the aggregate for bonus awards recognizing standout projects or community achievements. Those categories may include, without limitation, a community vote award, most viral project award, or other unique or notable project categories designated by the Organizers. The number, categories, allocation, and value of individual bonus prizes may be determined by the Organizers before or during the judging process. Any additional prize, including a public-vote prize, will be announced on or about November 10, 2026. Additional prizes do not reduce the track prizes above.

Total Prize Pool. The total anticipated prize pool for the Event is $1,000,000 U.S. dollars.
